Class Extraction Rules (Compressed)

General
- Extract shortest meaningful span (typically 1-6 words).
- Prefer single-concept labels; split composite phrases.

Type rules
- Business Activity: recurring actor-driven action/process.
- Indicator: measurable metric (rate, score, %, count, KPI, metric).
- Concept: abstract domain idea without explicit actor/action.
- Stakeholder: person/role/group/organization.
- Feature: system capability or provided function.
- Operational Data: concrete artifact/log/record or one-off event output.

Disambiguation
- If phrase includes actor + action, split into Stakeholder + Business Activity.
- Outcome/metric language prefers Indicator over Activity.
- One-off event/result should not be Business Activity.
- Implementation-heavy phrase should not be Concept.

Fallback order (when ambiguous)
- Indicator -> Business Activity -> Stakeholder -> Feature -> Operational Data -> Concept.
- If still unclear, keep conservative (Concept or REVIEW).
