[Canonical Label Selection Criteria] (in priority order)
1. Prefer official names over abbreviations (formal name over abbreviation, English terminology over casual terms)
   Example: 'Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare' over 'MoH', 'user' over 'app user'
2. Prefer terms defined in specifications or glossaries
3. Prefer more concise and general notation
   Example: 'user' over 'application user'
4. Prefer general forms without attributes or modifiers
   Example: 'user' over 'Japanese-speaking user'
5. Prefer shorter character count (when equivalent)
6. Merge synonyms across Stakeholder labels into a canonical form
   Example: 'Apple Inc.' and 'Apple' -> 'Apple Inc.'
7. Normalize duplicated high-level concept labels that refer to the same idea
   Example: 'privacy protection', 'data privacy' -> canonical 'privacy protection'
8. Create explicit parent-child relations where appropriate
   Example: 'Sports Organization' as the upper class of 'Nippon Professional Baseball' and 'J.League'
9. Normalize system-related device concepts
   Example: Normalize to canonical 'Device' with explicit subtypes for 'Smartphone' and 'Device' separately if necessary
10. Establish Infrastructure vs Device hierarchy
    Example: Distinguish 'cloud service provider-owned servers' as a System Component (Infrastructure) vs an Organization
