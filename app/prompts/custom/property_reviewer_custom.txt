### Relationship Validation Rules

- **exposedBy / exposes**: Source must be 'System Component'; target must be 'Feature' or 'Interface'. Reject if target is 'Business Requirement' or 'Stakeholder'.
- **realizeValue**: Destination must be a 'Value' instance; reject processes or activities.
- **hasBusinessRequirement**: Destination must link to a specific business outcome, not a generic system feature.
