### Class Validation Rules

- Business Activity: Must be a specific, repeatable verb-noun process. Reject broad or multi-process descriptions.
- Business Requirement: Must be a specific, testable need. Classify outcomes as Value.
- System Requirement: Must mandate a specific capability or constraint. Flag broad goals as Activity or Value.
- Value: Must be an outcome, benefit, or end-state. Reject functional needs.
- Analytical Insight: Must be a derived conclusion. Classify raw data as Operational Data.
- Vision vs. Feature: A Vision is a long-term strategic goal. A Feature is a specific, implementable capability. Reject specific functions as Visions.
