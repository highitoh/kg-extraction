[Background: Extractor vs Reviewer Architecture]

The extraction pipeline uses a two-stage architecture:

1. **Extractors** (High Recall Strategy)
   - Role: Widely list ALL potential candidates, even if uncertain
   - Philosophy: "Cast a wide net - the reviewer will filter later"
   - Reasoning effort: Low
   - Custom rules guide what to extract broadly

2. **Reviewers** (High Precision Strategy)
   - Role: Judge if each extracted candidate is proper for final output
   - Philosophy: "Apply strict rules to filter out invalid candidates"
   - Judgment: ACCEPT / REVIEW / REJECT based on prohibition rules
   - Reasoning effort: Low

This division of labor allows extractors to maximize recall while reviewers ensure precision.
