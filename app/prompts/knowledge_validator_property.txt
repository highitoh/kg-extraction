**Validation Perspectives:**

1. **Relation Applicability (適用性)** (Score 1-5)
   - Evaluates whether a relationship should be defined between the two connected classes.
   - Consider: Is this relationship meaningful and necessary? Should these two classes be connected in this way?

2. **Relation Unity (単一性)** (Score 1-5)
   - Evaluates whether the property does not semantically overlap with other relationships.
   - Consider: Is this relationship distinct from other properties? Does it represent a unique connection without duplicating other relationships?

3. **Relation Strength Validity (強度妥当性)** (Score 1-5)
   - Evaluates whether the determination of the property's relationship strength is appropriate.
   - Strength scale: 0 (invalid), 1 (weak), 2 (moderate), 3 (strong)
   - Consider: Is the assigned strength appropriate for this relationship? Does it accurately reflect the importance and strength of the connection in the domain?

4. **Contextual Grounding (文脈根拠性)** (Score 1-5)
   - Evaluates whether the property can be recognized as having a relationship from the context within the document.
   - Consider: Is there clear evidence in the document for this relationship? Can the connection be traced back to specific document content?

Evaluate ALL properties listed above and provide scores with detailed justifications for each perspective FOR EACH PROPERTY.

## Important Guidelines

1. **Scoring Scale**: Use a scale of 1-5 for all scores:
   - 1: Very Poor - Significant issues that require immediate attention
   - 2: Poor - Notable problems that should be addressed
   - 3: Acceptable - Meets minimum requirements but has room for improvement
   - 4: Good - Well-designed with minor areas for refinement
   - 5: Excellent - Exemplary quality with no significant concerns

2. **Justification**: Provide clear, specific, and actionable explanations for each score. Reference specific aspects of the property and how they relate to the evaluation criteria.

3. **Objectivity**: Base your evaluation on the criteria provided, not on personal preferences or assumptions outside the given context.

4. **Consistency**: Apply the same standards across all evaluations within this validation session.

5. **Language**: Respond in the same language as the label.
